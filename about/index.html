About

Built by a chemist with 30 years in the chemical industry — technology, product management, sales and marketing, and business development.

I spent 30+ years in specialty chemicals at ExxonMobil and Lubrizol — R&D, business development and innovation leadership, with 10 patents along the way. Now I run Bluetigre Consultants LLC, and I built the thing I always wanted as a BD leader: an AI engine that does the reading.

PROWL reads at machine speed and writes every fact down with its source. I make the judgements, with you. Nothing rests on one search or one person's memory, and nothing reaches you that a person hasn't approved.

The limits matter as much as the capability. PROWL does not size markets and does not rank commercial attractiveness — a high score means a market is well supported by evidence, not that it is worth your capital. That judgement is mine and yours, made together, on evidence you can check.

If you make or distribute specialty chemicals and want to know where else they could sell — let's talk.

How the work divides

BlueTigre brings the judgement. PROWL does the reading.
