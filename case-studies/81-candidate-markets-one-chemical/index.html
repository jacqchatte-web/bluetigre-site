The lead that did not survive

The top-ranked lead was refrigeration systems. PROWL read its own evidence back and rejected it: every document described the client's existing refrigeration-lubricant business, seen from the equipment end. Not white space — the same business under another name. The second application survived the same cut: a growing and important new use for the chemistry.

The three questions, answered

Is it real?Yes, for the chemistry.

The family is already sold commercially into that application, which proves it rather than predicts it. Unproven for the client's own grade — and the report says so.

Is it worth it?Register-verified, not brochure-claimed.

US volumes for the molecule and its family more than doubled since 2020, while imports of the family's trade basket fell 41% — import substitution, in two official sources.

Can we win?The file is clean; the field is not.

Clean regulatory standing on both sides of the Atlantic, with three new EU registrants since 2022. But the incumbents, the patents and the bio-based label sit with others. The window is measured in filings per year.

What did a week of PROWL actually produce here?
A sourced go-to-market foundation, two leads killed with the reasons on the record, and many more still to check. The killed leads are the evidence that the ranking is doing work — a list nothing falls off is a list nobody checked.
