The three questions, answered

Is it real?Yes — independent source classes agree.

The application exists at scale, and separate kinds of source say so independently rather than citing each other.

Is it worth it?Yes, but not as the molecule stands.

A known and partly patented processing step sits between the diol and the application. That is a real obstacle, and it is named rather than smoothed over.

Can we win?Yes — by changing who you sell to.

Sell the diol to the producers who do not make their own, on its sustainability credits. Of 114 players in the top category, 14 already buy rather than make.

Why is a patented processing step good news rather than bad?
Because it tells you which sale to make. The obstacle rules out selling the finished application, and rules in selling the diol to the 14 producers who already buy rather than make. A blocked market is still reported, together with what opening it would take.
