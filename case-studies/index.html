Case studies

What PROWL found — and what it rejected.

Both engagements are client-anonymous. Every figure traces back to the evidence record, and the leads that failed are here alongside the ones that survived, because the rejections are the part that shows the method works.

Established chemical

81 candidate markets, 39 the client already served — and the top-ranked lead was wrong

One established chemical. The highest-scoring lead turned out to be the client's own existing business seen from the other end, and PROWL rejected it on its own evidence. The second survived the same cut.

 Bio-based diol

51 candidate markets for a bio-based diol — and 14 buyers who would rather buy than make it

Real, and worth it — but not as the molecule stands. A known, partly patented processing step sits in the way, which changes who you sell to and what you sell them.
How are these case studies anonymised?
The client is never named and neither is the exact molecule where it would identify them. The figures, the sources and the reasoning are unchanged — what is removed is identity, not evidence.
