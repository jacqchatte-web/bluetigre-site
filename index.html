The problem

Your molecule could sell somewhere else. Finding where is a reading job nobody gets time for.

Scattered

Journals nobody in the commercial team reads. Patents from adjacent industries. Regulatory dockets that move a year before the announcement.

Three weeks of reading

A good BD manager would find some of it. No product line gets three weeks — he has a number this quarter.

So it goes elsewhere

The molecule stays in its lane. Somebody else's chemistry serves the market.

The alternatives are a report about a category rather than your molecule, a graduate with a search engine, or a consultancy that does not read chemistry. Why this happens, and what changes it →

Does PROWL ever get it wrong?
Yes, and the engagement is designed to catch it. In the first study the top-ranked lead was refrigeration systems. PROWL read its own evidence back and rejected it: every document described the client’s existing refrigeration-lubricant business, seen from the equipment end. Not white space — the same business under another name.
From the 81-market engagement. A finding you cannot check is a finding you should not act on.
