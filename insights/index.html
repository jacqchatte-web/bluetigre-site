Knowledge

Four kinds of knowledge. You keep all of them.

Most market work ends as a slide deck, and everything learned in making it is lost — so the next person starts from nothing. An engagement here ends as a structured record your company owns, in your own words, with every entry carrying where it came from and when it was read.

What gets built

Learned once, and inherited by everything after it.

GeneralTrue of the chemistry wherever it sells

Who supplies it and at what scale, how it stands with regulators on each side of the Atlantic, what safety credentials it already carries, which forms the market has settled on.

Every application you look at afterwards inherits this, so it is paid for once and used for years.

ProductWhat you make, in your words

What your company actually sells today, described the way your business describes it — your segment names, your brands and divisions, your own vocabulary.

Without it, the markets you already serve come back to you as discoveries. With it, they are subtracted before anything is ranked.

MarketEach candidate, and the facts behind it

Every market considered, with the facts that support or sink it — each one carrying its basis, its unit and its source, so a number can be traced rather than believed.

Including the markets that failed, and why. A list nothing falls off is a list nobody checked.

TechnicalWhat the substance actually does

Properties as measured and published, never as claimed. Interfacial behaviour, stability, compatibility — and how it behaves biologically, because that is often what decides where it can sell at all.

What makes it count

Three things go down with every finding, or it does not go down at all.

Where it came from

Without exception. If we cannot point at the document a claim came from, we do not write the claim down — not as a weaker fact, not at all.

What it does not prove

Written next to the finding, not in a footnote at the back. What the evidence will not support is part of the finding itself, not a disclaimer bolted on afterwards.

Where it stands

Still current, replaced by something later, or waiting on an answer only you can give. When something is replaced, the old version is kept and linked to the new one, so you can see what changed and when.

What happens when only you can answer a question?
It says so, in plain sight. Some things only you know — what a contract lets you do, what your plant can actually run. Those are set aside and marked as waiting for you, with the question written out and who it is for. They never sit among the things we have established, because showing a question as though it were an answer is a lie.
Same reason we record a search that found nothing differently from one that could not run at all.
The guardrail

The knowledge never feeds back into the searching.

This is the rule that keeps the record worth having. A conclusion a person has signed off is never used as evidence for the next search or the next score. If it were, the tool would start agreeing with itself. Its own earlier judgement would come back looking like independent support, and the second study would agree with the first for no reason at all.

This is wired in, not just intended. An automatic check refuses to let the work go ahead if the searching side can reach the conclusions at all. Nobody can cross that line by accident, or by not knowing the rule was there.
